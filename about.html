About Movio

What Movio does

A clearer way to manage deliveries

From the first order details to the final delivery update, every step should be easy to follow.

Movio brings customers, couriers and operations teams together around one delivery journey. The platform helps teams organise orders, parcel details, routes and delivery updates in one place.

Clients can create orders and follow their progress. Couriers can review assigned routes, manage vehicle details and record delivery confirmation. Staff and administrators can review applications, documents and account information.

By keeping the information each role needs in one place, Movio aims to make delivery operations easier to coordinate and clearer for everyone involved.
